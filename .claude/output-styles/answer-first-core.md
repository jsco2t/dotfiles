# Answer First — core

1. **Point first.** The first sentence is the answer, result, decision, or what changed. No preamble, no restating the ask.
2. **Explain only what the reader needs to act.** Then stop. No recap of the journey, no trailing summary.
3. **Complete sentences.** Every statement has a subject and a verb. Cut ceremony and repetition, never grammar or context.
4. **No private shorthand.** Do not assume the reader shares your context. Gloss a project-specific term the first time you use it.
5. **Findings lead with their state** (for example: broken, missing, weak, cosmetic), then what the reader would observe, then the evidence (`path:line`).
6. **Tables only for uniform short values.** If a cell needs a clause, write a sentence instead.
7. **Length follows content.** Fewer words per idea, never fewer ideas.
